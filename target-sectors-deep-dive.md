# StreamToday Studios: Target Sectors Deep Dive
## Nottingham/East Midlands Market Research Report

**Prepared:** April 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify high-value sectors and companies with marketing budget and appetite for long-form content production

---

## Executive Summary

This research identifies seven high-value sectors in Nottingham and the East Midlands where companies demonstrate both the budget and the strategic desire to invest in marketing and long-form content. The findings are based on company revenue data, growth indicators, funding rounds, hiring patterns, and existing content marketing activity.

**Key Finding:** The East Midlands is experiencing a business growth surge, with Nottingham ranking second in the UK for business growth potential. Companies across finance, life sciences, technology, and professional services are actively expanding marketing budgets, creating significant opportunity for StreamToday Studios.

---

## Sector 1: Financial and Business Services

### Sector Overview
Nottingham's financial and business services sector employs over 126,000 people. The city has established expertise in data and analytics, with companies like Experian, HD Decisions, and TDX Group based there. Major legal firms (Browne Jacobson, Geldards, Shoosmiths, Eversheds Sutherland, KPMG, Deloitte) have significant presence, alongside shared service operations for Capital One, Ikano Bank, and AmTrust.

### Target Companies

**1. Experian (Nottingham)**
- **Revenue:** £7.3bn+ globally (Nottingham is European HQ)
- **Content Marketing:** Extensive. Maintains "Marketing Forward Blog" and "Global News Blog" with data-driven marketing content. Produces video webinar series ("Experian Insights Video Series") and participates in podcasts discussing advertising data ecosystems, identity, and audience data.
- **Budget Signal:** Global marketing spend estimated in hundreds of millions. Actively investing in AI-powered marketing and data analytics content.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** High. Corporate podcast production, thought leadership video series, case study content.

**2. Oakbrook Finance (Nottingham)**
- **Revenue:** Estimated £15-20m+ (15% turnover growth 2021-2022)
- **Content Marketing:** Moderate. Operates consumer lending brands including Likely Loans. Digital-first marketing approach.
- **Budget Signal:** Raised £3.88m in equity funding (July 2022), post-money valuation £48.1m. Active expansion phase.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** Medium-High. Video content for brand storytelling, customer testimonial series.

**3. BFY Group (Nottingham)**
- **Revenue:** £8.3m (77% growth as of July 2024)
- **Content Marketing:** Limited evidence of long-form content.
- **Budget Signal:** High growth company, likely reinvesting in marketing. Consultancy serving energy, utilities, and financial sectors.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** Medium. Thought leadership podcast series for B2B professional services.

### Opportunity Rating: ★★★★☆

## Sector 2: Life Sciences and Healthcare

### Sector Overview
Nottingham has a history of breakthrough discoveries (Ibuprofen, MRI scanner) and employs over 91,000 people in life sciences. Strengths lie in medical devices, biotechnology, and healthcare. BioCity and MediCity provide business incubation. The sector is anchored by Boots UK and a growing cluster of medtech and biotech companies.

### Target Companies

**1. Boots UK (Nottingham)**
- **Revenue:** £7.3bn (FY 2024), up from £7bn previous year
- **Content Marketing:** Extensive and sophisticated. "TikTok-first ethos" with creator-driven advertisements (1.6x ROI vs graphic ads). YouTube presence with brand content, influencer partnerships, omnichannel campaigns. Uses podcasts as part of loyalty programme engagement. Heavy investment in video production.
- **Budget Signal:** 8.1% comparable retail sales growth, 23% surge in digital sales. Investing heavily in AI marketing and innovation.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** Lower (likely using global agencies). Good benchmark for content expectations.

**2. Upperton Pharma Solutions (Nottingham)**
- **Revenue:** £11.6m (76.77% increase, July 2024)
- **Content Marketing:** Limited. Company website with technical content.
- **Budget Signal:** High growth, likely expanding marketing presence. Specialises in spray drying technology for pharmaceutical ingredients.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** Medium. Technical B2B video, corporate storytelling, case study content.

**3. Oncimmune (Nottingham)**
- **Sector:** Biotechnology
- **Content Marketing:** Scientific publications, limited consumer-facing content.
- **Budget Signal:** Innovation-focused, likely engaging with media for research dissemination.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** Medium. Scientific documentary content, research highlight videos.

**4. CHAIN Biotechnology (Nottingham)**
- **Sector:** Biotechnology
- **Budget Signal:** Active in innovation space, funding received.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** Medium. Pitch videos, innovation storytelling.

### Opportunity Rating: ★★★★☆

**3. Reformed IT (Nottingham)**

- **Budget Signal:** Significant growth 2020-2023. Likely investing in marketing to sustain growth.
- **Fit for StreamToday:** Medium-High. Tech company content, corporate video, case studies.

**4. Blue Light Card (Cossington)**
- **Revenue:** 92% turnover growth (2021-2022)
- **Content Marketing:** Consumer-facing discount platform.
- **Budget Signal:** Raised equity funding February 2022 (Inflexion Buyout Fund) for R&D and product development.

## Content Marketing Gaps Identified

**Key Opportunities:**

1. **Thought Leadership Podcasts:** Many companies (especially B2B) lack dedicated podcast series despite having expertise to share
2. **Video Case Studies:** Strong demand across sectors for customer testimonial and case study video content
3. **Corporate Documentary:** Mid-sized companies increasingly want longer-form brand storytelling content
4. **Technical Video:** Manufacturing and SaaS companies need product demo and technical explanation videos
5. **Recruitment Video:** Several growing companies (True Position Robotics, Pentaxia) could benefit from employer brand content

---

## Budget Signals to Watch For

Based on this research, companies showing these signals are highest priority:

- Recent funding rounds (equity or debt)
- Featured in growth lists (FT 1000, Sunday Times Hundred)
- Hiring marketing roles (CMO, Marketing Director, Content Manager)
- Recent office expansion or relocation
- Acquisition activity (like Phenna Group's 34 acquisitions)
- Year-over-year revenue growth above 20%
