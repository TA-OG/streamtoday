## Email Optimisation Tactics

1. **Subject Lines:** Short, curiosity-driven, no sales language
   - Good: "Removing the edit pain from your content"
   - Bad: "Professional Podcast Studio in Nottingham - Free Trial"

2. **Personalisation:** First line must reference something specific about their company
   - "I noticed [Company] just launched [X]"
   - "Your LinkedIn post about [Y] resonated"
   - "Saw [Company] is hiring for [role] — growth is exciting"

3. **CTA:** Low friction. "Reply with availability" not "Book a call"

4. **Follow-up Timing:** Day 0, Day 3, Day 7. Three touches maximum.

5. **Tracking:** Monitor open rates, reply rates, pilot booking rate, conversion to retainer. Adjust copy based on data.
