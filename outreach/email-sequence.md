Marketing Directors, Managing Directors, Heads of Content at East Midlands companies (10-500 employees) in professional services, tech, recruitment, legal, finance, B2B sectors.

### Value Proposition
Professional podcast studio in Nottingham's Lace Market. Not a hobbyist setup — broadcast-quality audio/video with editing included. Companies use it for thought leadership, client acquisition, employer branding.

## Notes

- Personalisation is critical. Each Email 1 should reference something specific about the company (their website, LinkedIn, recent news).
- The free pilot is the hook. It removes friction and lets them experience the quality.
- The £549/mo retainer is positioned as a no-brainer compared to hiring in-house or using expensive agencies.
- Track: open rate, reply rate, pilot booking rate, conversion to retainer.
