# StreamToday Studios - Outreach Strategy

## Target Audiences

### 1. Nottingham Businesses
**Decision Makers:** Marketing Directors, Managing Directors, Communications Managers
**Location:** East Midlands (Nottingham, Derby, Leicester, Lincoln)
**Pain Points:**
- Want to do more video content but lack in-house capabilities
- Tried DIY content but quality is not good enough
- Need consistent content output for LinkedIn/social
- Budget for agency work but want more control

### 2. Social Media Personalities/Influencers
**Profile:** Local influencers, content creators, personal brands
**Location:** Nottingham and surrounding areas
**Pain Points:**
- Content quality holding back growth
- Need professional space for brand deals
- Want to level up production value
- Tired of recording at home

### 3. Sports Personalities
**Profile:** Athletes, coaches, sports professionals based in/around Nottingham
**Pain Points:**
- Want to build personal brand
- Need content for sponsorships
- No time to figure out tech
- Want to look professional without the hassle

## Instagram/TikTok Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Behind the Scenes
**Content:** Studio setup, equipment tours, recording sessions
**Goal:** Show professionalism and build trust
**Posting:** 2x per week

Examples:
- "This is what £50k of podcast gear looks like"
- "Before/after: Phone vs Studio lighting"
- "Day in the life: Recording 4 podcasts in one day"

### Pillar 2: Client Transformations
**Content:** Before/after client content, testimonials, case studies
**Goal:** Social proof and results
**Posting:** 1x per week

Examples:
- "How [Client] went from phone videos to BBC-quality interviews"
- Client testimonial videos
- "This content got 50k views - here's how we made it"

### Pillar 3: Educational
**Content:** Tips, tricks, how-to content
**Goal:** Position as experts, provide value
**Posting:** 2x per week

Examples:
- "3 lighting mistakes that make you look amateur"
- "How to sound confident on camera"
- "The 10-minute content batching system"

### Pillar 4: Nottingham Pride
**Content:** Local landmarks, Nottingham business shoutouts, Lace Market
**Goal:** Local connection and community
**Posting:** 1x per week

Examples:
- "Best coffee spots near the studio (for pre-recording caffeine)"
- "Why we chose Nottingham's Lace Market"
- Shoutouts to other local businesses

### Pillar 5: Direct Response
**Content:** Offers, availability, booking CTAs
**Goal:** Conversions
**Posting:** 1x per week

Examples:
- "This week's availability"
- "Last-minute slot open - who wants it?"
- "New client offer: 50% off your first session"
