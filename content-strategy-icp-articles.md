# StreamToday Content Strategy: ICP-Resonant Articles

**Goal:** Dominate search for "Podcast Studio [City/Industry]" and provide high-value "hand-raisers" for each target sector.
**Cadence:** 2 posts per week (drip-fed via cron).
**Voice:** Rory Sutherland-style — witty, contrarian, anti-equipment worship, pro-orchestration.

---

## Sector 1: Life Sciences & MedTech
**Target:** BioCity/MediCity tenants (Upperton, Sygnature, ValiRx).
**Pain Point:** Complex science is "locked" in PDFs; investors need to trust the team, not just the data.

### Article 1: "Why Your White Paper is Collecting Digital Dust (And What to Do About It)"
*   **Hook:** "You spent 40 hours writing it. Your investors spent 40 seconds skimming it. The problem isn't your science; it's your medium."
*   **Key Insight:** Audio is the only medium that captures the *passion* behind the discovery. It humanizes the lab coat.
*   **CTA:** Book a pilot episode to turn your latest grant update into a 20-minute authority builder.

### Article 2: "The BioCity Founder’s Guide to 'Humanizing' Hard Science"
*   **Hook:** "Your CTO is brilliant. But does he sound like a robot or a visionary? The difference is worth £5m in funding."
*   **Key Insight:** How to use long-form conversation to explain complex mechanisms without "dumbing them down."
*   **CTA:** See how Studio One’s multi-camera setup captures the nuance of technical debate.
